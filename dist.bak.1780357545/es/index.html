WHY THIS MATTERS

Korean climate policyis decided by citizens

Established May 16, 2026 following the Constitutional Court ruling — the first permanent national-level climate citizens assembly. 200 randomly selected citizens deliberate directly on 15 policy agendas.

Carbon Neutrality Act §19-2

PROCESS

4-Stage Deliberation

This wiki archives the entire process from citizen proposals to final recommendations.

STEP 1

Citizen Proposals

Anyone can propose agendas at climatevoice.kr. Tracking 31 proposals.

STEP 2

Planning Panel Workshop

Mixed expert-citizen panel refines and structures agendas.

STEP 3

9-Session Deliberation

200 participants deliberate over 9 sessions, 6 months. Direct use of En-ROADS and other tools.

STEP 4

Final Recommendations

November 2026 — citizen recommendations feed directly into national climate policy.

  • Real-time temperature scenarios to 2100
