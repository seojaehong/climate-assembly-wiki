# En-ROADS — 2026 Korea Climate Citizens' Assembly Wiki Section

This directory mirrors the En-ROADS climate simulator's official documentation and adds Korean-language facilitator materials for the **2026 Korea Climate Citizens' Assembly** (200 deliberating citizens — 20 planning + 180 deliberating-only — Article 19-2 of the Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth Framework Act).

## What is En-ROADS?

[En-ROADS](https://en-roads.climateinteractive.org/) is an interactive, system-dynamics climate simulator developed by **Climate Interactive**, **MIT Sloan**, and **Ventana Systems**. Users move 18 policy sliders and watch global temperature, energy mix, GDP, and health outcomes update in seconds. It is widely used in classrooms, parliaments, and citizens' assemblies worldwide.

## Audience

- **Korean facilitators** moderating the Assembly's breakout sessions (감축1/감축2/감축3/적응 tracks). Start at `ko/index.md`.
- **International researchers and journalists** observing Korea's deliberation. Start at `en/index.md`.
