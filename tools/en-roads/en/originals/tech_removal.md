# Technological Carbon Dioxide Removal

Methods to "pull significant amounts of carbon dioxide out of the atmosphere." Energy- and infrastructure-intensive.

## Primary Methods

**DACCS (Direct Air Capture and Storage)**: extracts CO2 from air for geological storage. "Captured carbon must be stored long term."

**Enhanced Mineralization**: processes basalt-type rocks to absorb atmospheric CO2 into stable minerals.

## Key Considerations
- **Development stage**: mostly pilots
- **Energy demands**: DACCS very energy-intensive — equity concerns
- **Permanence**: storage must prevent indefinite leakage
- **Industrial scale**: requires massive industrial capacity

## Co-Benefits / Trade-offs
Jobs from scaling, soil improvements from mineralization. But "many of the technological carbon removal approaches have not been developed at scale yet and pose unknown risks."

## Model Representation
Adjustable: subsidy levels, infra timelines, energy requirements, leakage rates (both DACCS and mineralization).
