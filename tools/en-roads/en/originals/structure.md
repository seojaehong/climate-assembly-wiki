# En-ROADS Structure

## Overview

The En-ROADS simulator operates on a straightforward framework: greenhouse gas concentrations drive global temperature increases, which subsequently trigger climate impacts including sea level rise and ocean acidification.

## Four Main Greenhouse Gas Sources

1. **Energy CO2 Emissions** — From burning coal, oil, gas, and biomass (~67% of total GHG emissions).
2. **Land Use CO2 Emissions** — From forestry and land use changes (~7%).
3. **Carbon Dioxide Removal** — Technologies/methods extracting CO2 to plants, soils, or geological formations.
4. **Other Greenhouse Gas Emissions** — Methane, N2O, F-gases (~26%).

## Energy Emissions Framework

Energy-related CO2 follows the **Kaya Identity**: population × GDP per capita × energy intensity × carbon intensity. "Reducing energy CO2 emissions is about four things: fewer people, less consumption, more efficiency, and less high-carbon energy supplies."

## Feedback Mechanisms

Climate impacts create feedback loops affecting economic growth and agricultural productivity, as rising temperatures increase costs from extreme weather, sea level rise, droughts, and flooding.
