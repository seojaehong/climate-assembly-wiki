# Buildings and Industry — Energy Efficiency

Increase or decrease efficiency in buildings, appliances, industrial machinery.

## Examples
- Insulation, efficient motors/lighting/appliances/HVAC, conservation
- Tax breaks, performance standards
- High-efficiency R&D

## Key Messages
High-leverage solution. Reducing demand cuts coal, oil, gas usage and lowers temperature.

## Key Dynamics
- **Impact**: all energy sources decline, especially electricity-related coal/gas
- **Demand**: overall consumption drops
- **Delay**: gradual — depends on stock turnover

## Co-Benefits
- Less air pollution, healthcare savings
- Lower bills, energy security
- Thermal comfort, grid resilience
- Retrofitting jobs

## Equity
- High upfront costs limit access for low-income/small businesses
- Renter-vs-owner policy gaps

## Slider Range
0% to +3% annual efficiency improvement. Status quo +1% to +1.7%.
