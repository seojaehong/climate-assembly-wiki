# Transport — Energy Efficiency

"Increase or decrease the energy efficiency of vehicles, shipping, air travel, and transportation systems."

## Examples
1. **Individual**: walking, biking, transit, carpooling, dense neighborhoods, efficient vehicles, less flying, telecommuting
2. **Policy**: parking pricing, transit investment, tax incentives, carpool rewards, bike infra, fuel efficiency standards
3. **Technology**: R&D for efficient shipping/vehicles/aircraft

## Main Messages
Notably reduces oil. "Less oil is burned, and coal and gas fall as well, as electrified transport becomes more efficient."

## Dynamics
- Fleet turnover delays transition
- Energy demand and consumer costs decline
- Oil drops noticeably

## Co-Benefits
- Air quality, health, productivity
- Lower fuel costs
- Less congestion/noise
- Physical activity benefits

## Equity
Infrastructure inequities (sidewalks/bike lanes in affluent areas); better mass transit expands lower-income mobility.

## Case Studies
- NYC: $10M school walking/biking → $230M health/safety benefits
- Barcelona: 20% car→bike shift → 38 deaths prevented/yr, -21,000 t emissions/yr
