# En-ROADS Tutorial

En-ROADS is designed to be an easy-to-use simulator for creating pathways to successfully address climate change globally. An 11-minute [introductory video tour](https://youtu.be/9ytkpO_pbWE) offers guidance on the model's interface features. For in-depth use, join Climate Interactive's free En-ROADS training program at [learn.climateinteractive.org](https://learn.climateinteractive.org/).

## Graphs

There are over 100 output graphs available in En-ROADS, updating as you move sliders.

- **Select graphs** — Click the title of the left or right graph, or use the Graphs menu in the top toolbar.
- **More info** — Click the triangle icon to the left of the graph title.
- **Copy graph data** — Three-dots menu → "Copy Data to Clipboard."
- **Shortcut to popular graphs** — "Show miniature graphs" icon on the top toolbar.
- **View larger graphs** — Three-dots menu → "View Larger" or "View in New Window."

## Sliders / Actions

There are 19 sliders representing actions you can test. Each slider's detail view shows:

- A description of the slider
- Numeric values along the slider; you can enter exact values
- Related graphs
- Help / linked User Guide entry

![Slider interface](IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER_https://docs.climateinteractive.org/projects/en-roads/en/latest/_images/tutorial/sliders.b98549e242.jpg)

## Top Toolbar Features

- **Share Your Scenario** — Generates a shareable link including last viewed graphs.
- **Replay Last Change** — Replays the most recent slider move for inspection.
- **Assumptions** (Simulation menu) — Access and adjust model assumptions.
- **U.S. Units** (View menu) — Toggle metric / U.S. units.
- **Actions and Outcomes** (View menu) — Summary of your scenario's actions and outcomes.
- **Related Examples** (Help menu) — Common examples per slider.
