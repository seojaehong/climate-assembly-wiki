# Deforestation and Mature Forest Degradation

"Encourage or discourage the protection of forests." Deforestation = converting forest to other uses (mostly agriculture). Degradation = temporary forest loss from harvesting.

## Examples
- Protected-area policies; restrictions on soy/palm oil
- Indigenous land rights
- Public campaigns for preservation

## Key Messages
"Deforestation emissions are overshadowed by the enormous amount of carbon dioxide released through fossil fuel combustion." Still — biodiversity, Indigenous rights matter.

## Dynamics
- Deforestation driven by population/food consumption
- Degradation driven by bioenergy/lumber/paper harvest
- Higher crop yields reduce pressure for land expansion
- Climate change itself reduces yield growth

## Co-Benefits
Biodiversity, ecosystem services, erosion/water quality, sustainable livelihoods.

## Equity
Forest preservation has restricted Indigenous land access — "policies created with local stakeholder engagement" essential.
