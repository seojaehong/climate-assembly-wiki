# Population Growth

**Assume higher or lower population growth.** Population significantly drives GHG emissions (closely tied to consumption). Women's education and reproductive health access can accelerate the transition to smaller families.

## Examples
- Different fertility/demographic assumptions
- Empowerment of women and girls → lower fertility
- Reproductive health education and services

## Big Messages
- Limiting population growth is **not** a silver bullet.
- Population/family decisions are personal — ethical implications.

## Key Dynamics
- **Impact**: energy demand depends on population — observe all sources change
- **Delay**: population shifts play out over decades

## Co-Benefits (Lower Growth)
- Lower resource consumption
- Better women's quality of life and income

## Equity
- Policies must be voluntary, empowering women
- Many women of color in countries with structural inequities — investment needed there
- Forced sterilization of women of color has historical precedent — must never be encouraged
