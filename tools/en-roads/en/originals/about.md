# About En-ROADS

## Overview

En-ROADS is an interactive simulation tool designed for exploring global energy and climate policy solutions. According to the guide, it enables users to "create scenarios that focus on how changes in taxes, subsidies, economic growth, energy efficiency, technological innovation, carbon pricing, fuel mix, and other factors will change global carbon emissions and temperature."

## Key Characteristics

- **Speed**: Returns results within seconds
- **Transparency**: Clear mathematical logic underlying calculations
- **Interactivity**: Allows testing of hundreds of factors
- **Accessibility**: Works well for group-based decision-making across government, business, and civil society sectors

## Development & Leadership

En-ROADS (Energy-Rapid Overview and Decision-Support) was developed by Climate Interactive in collaboration with Tom Fiddaman of Ventana Systems, MIT Sloan's John Sterman, and UMass Lowell's Juliette Rooney-Varga. It builds upon the earlier C-ROADS simulator using system dynamics modeling methodology.

## Scope & Applications

While originating as an energy-focused tool, En-ROADS now encompasses land use, economics, and public policy. The model emphasizes "system-wide interactions of policies" and reveals how different levers interconnect — for instance, how energy efficiency impacts renewable energy adoption.

The tool complements larger disaggregated models used in the Integrated Assessment Modeling Consortium for calibration and comparison purposes.
