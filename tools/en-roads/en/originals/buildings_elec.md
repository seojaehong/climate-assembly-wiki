# Buildings and Industry — Electrification

"Increasing the use of electricity or hydrogen, instead of fuels like oil or gas, in buildings, appliances, heating systems, and other machines."

## Key Points
- **Effectiveness** depends on low-carbon electricity sources (solar, wind).
- **Best paired** with renewables or fossil discouragement.

## Critical Dynamics
1. **Fuel Switching** — oil drops (oil is primarily fuel), coal less (it serves both)
2. **Renewable Integration** — accelerates renewable deployment
3. **Time Delays** — equipment replaced over decades

## Co-Benefits
- Air quality, health, productivity
- No gas infra → fewer fire/explosion risks
- Less noise

## Equity
Retrofitting costs are barriers for low-income/small business; indoor air pollution correlated with poverty.

## Implementation
Subsidies 0–50% of electric equipment cost.
