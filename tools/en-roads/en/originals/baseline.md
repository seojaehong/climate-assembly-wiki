# En-ROADS Baseline Scenario

## Overview

The En-ROADS Baseline Scenario models the world's trajectory if "societal and technological changes were to continue at their current rate" without new policies. It serves as a reference point for testing climate interventions, though it's not intended as a prediction.

## What's Included

Aggregate effects of current global conditions: technological advancement, existing policies, and investment patterns. Real-world initiatives such as the U.S. Inflation Reduction Act and China's "1+N" framework are reflected through their combined influence on renewable energy promotion, fossil fuel subsidies, and efficiency improvements — assumed to persist without intensification or reversal.

## What's Excluded

Pledged net-zero commitments, countries' Paris Agreement contributions (NDCs), and long-term continuations of national-level policies. Announced commitments frequently change. Users can independently model NDC impacts by comparing scenarios against the baseline.

## Customization

Modify parameters via *Simulation > Assumptions* to adjust climate, economic, land use, and energy system variables for sensitivity analysis.
