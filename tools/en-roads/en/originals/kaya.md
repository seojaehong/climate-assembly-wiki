# Kaya Graphs

The Kaya Graphs illustrate the components driving growth in CO2 emissions from energy (~two-thirds of all GHG emissions). View via *View > Kaya Graphs*. Named after Yoichi Kaya:

> Global Population × GDP per Capita × Energy Intensity of GDP × Carbon Intensity of Energy = CO2 Emissions from Energy

## Key Factors

**Global Population** — Now exceeds 8 billion; projected ~10.2 billion by 2100 (UN). Growth slowing.

**GDP per Capita** — Steady annual growth, driven by rising living standards in China, India, South Africa, Mexico, Brazil, Indonesia.

**Energy Intensity of GDP** — Declining as economies become more efficient and shift from manufacturing to services.

**Carbon Intensity of Final Energy** — Modest decline as the global energy mix gradually shifts to low-carbon sources.

**CO2 Emissions Outcome** — Baseline scenario emissions continue rising. "Improvements in efficiency and decarbonization are not yet keeping up with the strong growth in population and consumption."
