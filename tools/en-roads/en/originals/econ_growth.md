# Economic Growth

Measured in GDP per person — key driver of energy consumption.

## Examples
- Global initiatives reducing consumption / voluntary simplicity
- High economic growth → more resource use and emissions

## Big Messages
- Slower growth is high-leverage but raises equity questions
- Climate impacts can themselves slow growth

## Key Dynamics
- GDP per capita × population = global GDP (Gross World Product)
- Decarbonized energy reduces temperature impact of growth
- Damage feedback loop: warming → less growth → less emissions (can be disabled via assumptions)

## Co-Benefits of Lower Growth
- Focus on alternative prosperity (gross national happiness)
- Conservation, less waste

## Equity
- Growth historically reduces poverty (recent gains skewed wealthy)
- Lower growth can mean austerity, job losses
- Climate action reduces damage → higher GDP → more demand

## Slider Settings
- Long-term: 0.5% to 2.5% annually
- Near-term: 1.7% to 3.7% annually
