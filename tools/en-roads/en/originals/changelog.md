# En-ROADS Model Version History

## May 2026 — v26.5.0

**Interface**
- Bahasa Indonesia translation
- Updated hydrogen subsidy sliders (transport + buildings/industry) to include consumer-facing fuel subsidies
- New "Price after consumer subsidies" line on hydrogen pricing graph
- New "Hydrogen" wedge in energy supply subsidies viz

**Model**
- Disaggregated heat-transfer electric equipment + technical feasibility improvements
- Enhanced hydrogen carrier market structure
- Shifted from radiative forcing to **effective** radiative forcing calculations

## April 2026 — v26.4.0

Major update to energy subsidy and tax policy modeling.

- Standardized supply sliders (Coal, Oil, Gas, Bioenergy, Renewables, Nuclear) with consistent units
- Units changed to "% of price at source" or "% of levelized cost"
- Independent tax and subsidy sliders per source
- Bioenergy feedstock sliders → disaggregated price adjustment controls
- New financial graphs: Net Revenue, Energy Supply Subsidies, Fossil Fuel Revenues
- Updated price graphs reflecting historical taxes/subsidies
- New Ocean Systems submodel (alkalinity, disaggregated climate sectors)
- Historical EV subsidies reflected in baseline

## March 2026 — v26.3.0
- Updated NOAA radiative forcing data through 2024

## February 2026 — v26.2.0
- Bangla language support
- New graph: "% Final Energy Consumption by Carrier"

## January 2026 — v26.1.0
- New anthropogenic + biogenic methane emissions graph
- Nuclear infrastructure reduction slider
- Subsidy start-year sliders for transport and buildings electrification
- Energy efficiency slider ranges adjusted to 0.0%–3.0% annually
- Updated UNEP, PRIMAP, IEA, Global Carbon Budget data through 2024–2025
