# New Zero-Carbon Energy Technology

Models a hypothetical breakthrough zero-emissions electricity source — fusion, thorium, SMRs, etc.

## Key Dynamics
- **Long commercialization timeline**: 20 years to commercialize plus planning/permit/construction
- **Market competition**: "crowding out" renewables/nuclear
- **Price-driven adoption**: if cheaper than coal, demand spikes — but slow effect on near-term emissions

## Controllable Parameters
- Breakthrough year
- Time to commercialize
- Initial cost relative to coal (1–2×)

## Main Message
Even optimal new tech takes decades to displace fossil fuels — limits near-term impact and competes with existing low-carbon options.
