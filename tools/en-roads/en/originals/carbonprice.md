# Carbon Pricing and Energy Standards

Global carbon price adjusts energy costs by their CO2 emissions.

## Key Policy Approaches
- Carbon taxes on producers
- Clean Electricity Standards (% from qualifying sources)
- Emissions Performance Standards (CO2/unit)
- Cap-and-trade (tradeable allowances)

## Main Mechanisms

**Energy Source Response** — Coal drops most (carbon-intensive); gas moderately; oil minimally; renewables become more competitive.

**Demand Effects** — Higher energy costs reduce overall consumption.

**Methane Reduction** — Gas pricing incentivizes leak prevention.

## Co-Benefits & Equity
Pros: renewable jobs, cleaner air, health. Cons: low-income cost pressures, worker transition needs, possible industry loopholes.
