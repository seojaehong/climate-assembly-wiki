# Natural Gas

Used for electricity, heating, industry. Burning releases CO2 (less than coal/oil), but methane can leak. Drilling consumes water and risks contamination.

## Examples of Discouraging
- Subsidy removal, taxes, anti-fracking laws
- Financial institutions restricting capital

## Big Messages
- "More natural gas is not an effective long-term strategy for the climate."
- Gas infrastructure has long lifespans competing with lower-carbon alternatives.

## Key Dynamics
- **"Squeezing the Balloon"**: discouraging gas alone may shift demand to coal — combine with carbon pricing.
- **Methane Leakage**: taxing gas incentivizes leak repairs.
- **Price-Demand Feedback**: efficiency vs. low-income impacts.

## Co-Benefits
- Reduced drilling → water security, habitat protection
- Fracking bans → health/environmental

## Equity
Natural gas production disproportionately affects low-income/minority communities.

## Slider Settings
- Highly discouraged: +200% to +100%
- Status quo: ~30% subsidy
- More encouraged: -35% to -50%

## FAQs
Advanced: "Reduce new gas infrastructure," "% Reduction in gas utilization," methane leakage adjustments. CCS via subsidies or carbon pricing.
