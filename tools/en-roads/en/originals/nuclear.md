# Nuclear

**Encourage or discourage building nuclear power plants.** Nuclear produces no CO2 but produces harmful nuclear waste.

## Examples
**Discouraging**: public campaigns on risk, retire existing plants.
**Encouraging**: policies for waste management and cost reduction; corporate promotion of acceptance.

## Big Messages
- Nuclear has not seen the cost reductions of wind/solar; remains expensive.
- Could be part of a suite of climate actions if one accepts environmental costs (waste, radiation risk).

## Key Dynamics
- **Impact**: subsidies grow Nuclear, shrink Coal/Gas in primary energy graph.
- **"Crowding Out"**: nuclear competes with renewables.
- **Delays**: subsidies phase in over 10 years; construction takes years.
- **Electrification** amplifies nuclear's impact.

## Co-Benefits of Discouraging
- Lower radiation/meltdown risk
- Less water use → habitat protection
- Reduced mining harm

## Equity
- Plants, mines, waste sites often in low-income/marginalized communities
- Uranium mining poses worker/community health risks

## Slider Settings

| Level | % of levelized cost |
|-------|--------------------|
| Highly discouraged | +50% to +25% |
| Discouraged | +25% to 0% |
| Status quo | 0% to -35% |
| More encouraged | -35% to -65% |
| Highly encouraged | -65% to -100% |

Nuclear is currently subsidized in many parts of the world (~30% subsidy status quo). To simulate removal, move to 0%.

## Model Structure
Tracks capacity stages: under development, under construction, producing, with delays.

## FAQs
- **SMRs**: simulate via the **New Zero-Carbon** slider rather than Nuclear.
