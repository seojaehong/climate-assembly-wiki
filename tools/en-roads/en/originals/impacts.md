# Impacts of Climate Change

## Overview

Climate impacts encompass effects on people, economies, ecosystems, infrastructure: "reduced crop yields, more deaths due to extreme heat, lower economic growth," driven by temperature, CO2 concentration, sea level rise.

## Impact Categories

**Atmosphere** — Temperature Change, Temperature Change by Location Map, CO2 Concentration, Methane Concentration, GHG Concentration.

**Extreme Weather** — Extreme Heat Days/Humidity Maps, Frequency of Extreme Heat, Deaths from Extreme Heat (by region), Outdoor Labor Losses, Population Exposure to Hurricanes/Typhoons/Flooding.

**Health and Economy** — Air Pollution, Malaria/Dengue Exposure, Crop Yield Decrease, Crop Nutrient Decrease, Global GDP Loss.

**Oceans** — Sea Level Rise/Flood Risk Maps, Population Exposure to SLR, Ocean Acidification, Ice-free Arctic Probability, Ocean Life Loss.

**Ecosystems & Biodiversity** — Drought Likelihood, Wildfire Danger Days, Arid Land Expansion, Ecosystem Shifts, Species Extinction Risk, Climate Range Loss for Endemic Species.

## Key Messages

Climate impacts often accelerate nonlinearly — "they can become much worse very quickly" with small temperature increases. Mitigation creates co-benefits visible in 2030–2040 projections.

## Primary Mechanisms

- **Temperature** drives most impacts via SLR, cyclone intensification, habitat destabilization.
- **CO2 concentration** causes ocean acidification.
- **Air pollution** from fossil combustion improves immediately with clean energy transitions.

## Equity

Climate change disproportionately affects low-income communities, racial minorities, the elderly, people with disabilities.
