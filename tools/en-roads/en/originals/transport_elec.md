# Transport — Electrification

Adoption of electric cars, trucks, buses, trains, hydrogen ships/aircraft. Emissions reductions depend on low-carbon electricity (solar, wind).

## Examples
Charging infra, battery/vehicle R&D, corporate EV commitments, purchase rebates.

## Main Messages
Most effective combined with renewables encouragement or fossil discouragement.

## Core Dynamics
- **Efficiency**: EVs use less energy than ICE
- **Energy Mix Changes**: oil ↓, electricity ↑ (coal/renewables/gas)
- **Renewables Integration**: drives renewable growth
- **Turnover Delays**: fleets replace over decades

## Co-Benefits
- Better air quality
- Battery/EV manufacturing jobs

## Equity
- Affordability/availability gaps
- Lithium/copper mining damage
- Charging infrastructure inequality

## Slider Range
Subsidies 0%–50% of purchase cost. Baseline ~7%.
