# Agricultural Emissions and Food Choices

"Methane (CH4) arises from sources like cows and rice paddies, while nitrous oxide (N2O) comes from fertilizers and manure."

## Reducing Methane
- Livestock diet/health optimization
- Anaerobic digesters
- Rice water management
- Crop rotation

## Reducing N2O
- Precision/controlled-release fertilizers
- Drip irrigation
- Nitrification inhibitors

## Main Takeaways
"Improving agricultural practices can substantially decrease the amount of methane and nitrous oxide produced per unit of agricultural products." But agricultural emissions cannot be fully eliminated. Methane + N2O ≈ 22% of current GHG emissions.

## Co-Benefits
Food security, water quality, less ground-level ozone.

## Equity
Changes can threaten food security and farming-dependent communities — local context essential.
