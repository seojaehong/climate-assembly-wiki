# En-ROADS Glossary

**Afforestation**: Starting a forest or stand of trees in an area where there was no forest before.

**AIM/CGE**: An Integrated Assessment Model maintained by the National Institute for Environmental Studies, Japan.

**Anthropogenic**: Caused by human activity.

**AR5**: IPCC Fifth Assessment Report on Climate Change (2014).

**AR6**: IPCC Sixth Assessment Report on Climate Change (2021 and 2022).

**BECCS**: Bioenergy with carbon capture and storage. An experimental method of energy generation and technological carbon dioxide removal.

**Biochar**: A form of charcoal produced from plant matter and added to soil as a carbon dioxide removal method and soil nutrient enhancer.

**Biomass**: Organic (carbon-based) material that comes from living organisms such as plants and can be used as fuel.

**BOE (barrel of oil equivalent)**: A unit of energy approximately equivalent to burning 1 barrel of oil (159 L) or 6.12 GJ.

**Capital stock turnover**: The time it takes for physical energy infrastructure to be retired and replaced.

**Carbon intensity**: The amount of carbon dioxide emitted per amount of energy.

**CCS**: Carbon capture and storage. CO2 emissions are captured at the source and stored where they will not leak.

**CDR**: Carbon dioxide removal. Pulling CO2 from atmosphere via technology (e.g., DAC) or plants (e.g., afforestation).

**CH4**: Methane. A greenhouse gas from cows, agriculture, natural gas drilling, waste.

**Climate change**: Long-term changes in Earth's weather patterns.

**Climate change adaptation**: Changes by people, plants, animals in response to climate change.

**Climate sensitivity**: The amount of global surface temperature increase per doubling of CO2.

**CO2**: Carbon dioxide. Greenhouse gas naturally made by living things or produced by burning fuel.

**Co-benefit**: A positive non-climate effect of climate action (e.g., better air from coal phaseout).

**C-ROADS**: Climate-Rapid Overview and Decision Support simulator focusing on emission reduction pledges.

**Crop yield**: Food/feed produced per hectare of farmland.

**Damage function**: Estimated effect of temperature change on global economic growth.

**Deforestation**: Clearing of trees, transforming a forest into cleared land.

**DACCS**: Direct air carbon capture and storage. CO2 captured from air with machines and stored permanently.

**EIA**: U.S. Energy Information Administration.

**EMF**: Stanford Energy Modeling Forum.

**Emissions**: Making and giving off something (e.g., CO2).

**En-ROADS**: Energy Rapid Overview and Decision-Support climate change solutions simulator by Climate Interactive.

**Equity**: Creating conditions that enable just and fair inclusion of everyone into society.

**Exajoule**: 10^18 joules.

**F-gases**: Fluorinated gases (synthetic) — industrial uses; powerful GHGs.

**Feedstock**: Raw material for an energy or industrial process.

**Final consumption of energy sources**: Total energy consumed plus feedstocks for products.

**Fossil fuels**: Coal, oil, natural gas. Derived from ancient plants and animals.

**GCAM**: An Integrated Assessment Model (PNNL/JGCRI).

**GDP**: Gross Domestic Product.

**Gigajoule**: 10^9 joules.

**GISTEMP**: GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (NASA).

**Greenhouse gas**: Any gas that absorbs Earth's radiation and traps heat.

**Gtons**: Gigatons (10^9 tons or 10^12 kg).

**GWP**: Global warming potential — heat absorbed by a GHG vs equivalent CO2.

**HadCRUT5**: Met Office Hadley Centre historical surface temperature dataset.

**HFCs**: Hydrofluorocarbons — an F-gas type (refrigeration, A/C).

**HVAC**: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning.

**IAM**: Integrated Assessment Model. Links economic activity with biological/geophysical dynamics.

**IEA**: International Energy Agency.

**IMAGE**: IAM (PBL Netherlands).

**IPCC**: Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change.

**Joule**: Unit of energy (~1 J = lifting an apple 1 m).

**Kaya graphs**: Show drivers of CO2 emission growth (Population × GDP/capita × Energy intensity × Carbon intensity).

**kWh**: Kilowatt hour.

**Mature forest degradation**: Harvesting older forests for bioenergy/lumber/paper that disturbs forest and releases C.

**MCF**: Thousand cubic feet (natural gas volume).

**MESSAGE-GLOBIOM**: IAM (IIASA).

**Multisolving**: Cross-sector cooperation to address multiple problems with one policy/investment.

**MWh**: Megawatt hour = 1000 kWh.

**N2O**: Nitrous oxide — GHG.

**NF3**: Nitrogen trifluoride (F-gas).

**NGFS**: Network for Greening the Financial System.

**Paris Agreement**: 2015 international treaty (196 countries) — limit warming to well below 2 °C.

**PFCs**: Perfluorinated chemicals (F-gases).

**PM2.5**: Particulate matter ≤ 2.5 μm.

**ppm**: Parts per million.

**Primary energy demand**: Total energy in raw source before conversion or feedstock use.

**Progress ratio**: Cost reduction per doubling of cumulative production (~20% for renewables).

**RCP**: Representative concentration pathway (IPCC).

**REMIND-MAgPIE**: IAM (Potsdam Institute).

**Radiative forcing (RF)**: Energy absorbed by Earth minus energy radiated to space.

**SF6**: Sulfur hexafluoride (F-gas).

**SSPs**: Shared Socioeconomic Pathways — five future narratives.

**Terajoule**: 10^12 joules.

**Thorium**: Element usable as nuclear fission fuel.

**TOE**: Ton of oil equivalent = 29.3 GJ.

**WEO**: World Energy Outlook (IEA annual).

**WITCH-GLOBIOM**: IAM (European Institute on Economics and the Environment).
