# Oil

Widely used in transport, industry, heating, and electricity. Model policies discouraging or encouraging oil through this slider.

## Key Findings

**Difficulty of Replacement** — "Oil demand is more resistant to changes in price" due to portability and energy density. Transitioning typically requires electrification (e.g., EVs).

**The Balloon Effect** — Taxing oil alone shifts demand to coal/gas. Solution: restrict all fossil fuels or implement carbon pricing.

**Fuel Switching** — Higher oil prices encourage EV adoption — shifting demand to electricity (whose source mix matters).

## Policy

**Co-Benefits**
- Fewer spills protecting ecosystems
- Reduced oil dependence improves energy security

**Equity Challenges**
- Workforce transition in technical trades
- Refineries historically concentrated in marginalized communities
- Industry political/economic influence

## Slider Settings
- Highly discouraged: +200%
- Status quo: +20% to 0%
- Highly encouraged: -50%
