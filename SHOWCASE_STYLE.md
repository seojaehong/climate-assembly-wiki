# SHOWCASE_STYLE.md — Korean Showcase Style Guide

> Purpose: Translated content (EN/JA/ZH/ES) on this wiki is **not a literal translation** of the Korean originals. It is an English/Japanese/Chinese/Spanish **showcase of how Korea is approaching climate citizens' deliberation** — for international researchers, policymakers, and journalists.

## 5 Principles (binding for all translated agenda·session·doc pages)

### 1. Lede = Korean Locator
The opening paragraph MUST tell the reader, in one sentence, that this is from Korea's 2026 Climate Citizens' Assembly (220 members, established under Article 19-2 of the Carbon Neutrality Framework Act).

> ✅ "Korea's first national Climate Citizens' Assembly (2026, 220 members) is exploring..."
> ❌ "Climate vulnerability is a complex concept that..."

### 2. Korea-Specific Anchors
Use uniquely Korean examples wherever possible:
- 2022 Seoul semi-basement (banjiha) flood deaths
- 2024 multi-month heatwave (April–September)
- Outdoor workers (gig delivery, construction, agricultural laborers)
- 2017 Shin-Kori 5/6 deliberative poll (proven domestic precedent)
- Gyeonggi Climate Citizens' Assembly (province-level model, 120+330 members, 90% adoption rate)
- Professor Park Chan (University of Seoul) Session 1 framing

### 3. International Comparison = "What Korea Learned + What Korea Is Trying New"
Avoid "Korea is copying Canada/UK." Use evolution framing:
> ✅ "Inspired by Canada's Climate Dividend (2019–), Korea's Assembly is exploring whether a tailored adaptation could address Korea's specific structural inequalities…"
> ❌ "Korea wants to copy Canada's policy."

### 4. Cite Laws, Statutes, Statistics
Always name the specific legal/statistical anchor:
- "Article 19-2 of the Carbon Neutrality Framework Act"
- "11th Basic Plan for Electricity Supply and Demand (2024–2038)"
- "2030 NDC: 40% reduction from 2018 levels"
- "2018 baseline emissions: 727.6 Mt CO₂eq"
This makes the wiki citable in academic and policy contexts.

### 5. Moderator Caution — Present Tense, Tentative
Citizens' Assembly recommendations are not yet government policy. Use:
> ✅ "The Assembly is currently considering..."
> ✅ "Proposals under discussion include..."
> ❌ "Korea will introduce..." (unless an actual law/regulation exists)
> ❌ "Korea has decided..." (unless a vote has occurred)

## Page Structure (recommended for agenda pages)

```
1. Locator paragraph (1 sentence — see Principle 1)
2. Why this matters for Korea (Korea-specific context)
3. The proposal in plain language
4. International precedent (with "what Korea learned + adapts")
5. Open questions the Assembly is deliberating
6. Related: Sessions where this came up, related agendas, relevant ministries
7. Citation block (suggested format: [Project]. (2026). [Agenda]. Korea Climate Assembly Wiki. https://climate-assembly.org/[lang]/agenda/[slug])
```

## Length Guideline
- Agenda pages: 600–1200 English words
- Session pages: 400–800 words
- Doc/About pages: 400–600 words
