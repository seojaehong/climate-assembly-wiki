## What This Site Is

This site is the **open archive of an individual moderator** working with **Korea's first national Climate Citizens' Assembly (2026)** — a body of 220 randomly-selected citizens convened to learn, deliberate, and recommend national climate policy.

The Assembly is the **world's first permanent national-level climate deliberative body**, established under **Article 19-2 of Korea's Framework Act on Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth in Response to the Climate Crisis** (Carbon Neutrality Framework Act). It launched on 16 May 2026.

## Why This Exists

Three problems motivated this archive:

1. **The "one-off public forum" problem.** Citizens invest significant time crafting recommendations, but what happens to those recommendations afterward — and what the next cohort of citizens should reference — is often opaque.
2. **International invisibility of the Korean model.** The UK, Ireland, and France climate assemblies are well-documented in English-language research. The Korean model has, until now, **no primary English-language reference material**. OECD, UNFCCC, and international researchers have had no citable source for Korea's approach.
3. **Privatization of moderator know-how.** How facilitators run small-group deliberation, respond to citizen questions, and synthesize agendas tends to live only in individual moderators' heads.

This archive is **one individual's attempt** to address all three at once — open, citable, multilingual, durable.

## What You'll Find

- **15 agenda wiki pages** — Korean context, international comparisons, and open questions for each topic under deliberation
- **Session timeline** — chronological record of lectures, deliberations, and recommendation development
- **Moderator's brief** — facilitation methodology, citizen response patterns, checklists
- **Ministry roles matrix** — how 12 Korean ministries divide mitigation and adaptation responsibilities
- **Comparative analyses** — Gyeonggi Climate Citizens' Assembly (province-level precedent), Shin-Kori 5/6 deliberative poll (2017), UK, Ireland, France
- **5 languages** — Korean (source) + English + Japanese + Chinese + Spanish

## Multilingual Policy — Author-Time Generation

All English, Japanese, Chinese, and Spanish pages on this wiki are written and reviewed by the moderator **at authoring time** with LLM assistance — not post-build machine translation. Trust labels indicate verification depth:

- 🟢 `author-verified` — Reviewed directly by the moderator (Korean and English baseline)
- 🔵 `reviewed` — LLM-assisted with moderator's general review (Japanese, Chinese, Spanish initial pages)
- 🟢 `native-verified` — Approved by a native-speaker volunteer reviewer (gradually accumulating)
